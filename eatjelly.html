How the afternoon runs

01

Base and color

Mix and set your clear agar base, then choose from small-batch dyed jelly for your flowers.

02

Sculpt the flowers

Learn needle and cane techniques to build petals, leaves, and layers inside the gel.

03

Set and take home

Your piece chills while we pack it for travel. Walk out with your own jelly sculpture.
